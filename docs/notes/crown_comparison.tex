\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Comparison of the \texttt{--crown} selection applied to the Delphes samples against the
ntuple-level selection in \texttt{KIT-CMS/BBTauTauAnalysis-CROWN}
(\texttt{nmssm\_config.py}). This is a \emph{different stage} from the published
HIG-25-008 analysis selection: CROWN keeps $\pt>20$ on all three legs and $\tauh$
candidates at the \textbf{VVVLoose} working point, applying the trigger thresholds,
the elliptical signal region and the categorisation only later. Comparing our
analysis-level selection against their ntuple-level one is what produced an apparent
factor $\sim$5 discrepancy in signal efficiency; the correct comparison is this one.
\end{abstract}

\section{Where the residual efficiency gap lives}

\begin{description}\itemsep3pt
\item[$\tauh$ working point --- the dominant term.] CROWN keeps $\tauh$ candidates at
  VVVLoose and applies Medium later in the analysis, precisely so the misidentification
  background can be estimated from the looser sample. A Delphes $\tauh$ carries a
  \emph{single bit} already fixed at a Medium-equivalent working point: there is no
  looser candidate to keep, because it was never written. \textsc{DeepTau} Medium is
  $\sim$70\% efficient where VVVLoose is $\sim$98\%, so a factor $\sim$1.4 on the
  $\tauh$ leg is expected and cannot be recovered by any selection change. This is the
  same root cause as the $\tauh$-object rebuild on the tuning-v2 critical path.
\item[Opposite charge --- works the other way.] We keep same-sign pairs that CROWN
  rejects. Same-sign is CMS's QCD control region at $\sim$99\% purity, i.e.\ an
  essentially pure fake-$\tau$ population, so \emph{our background is inflated} by its
  absence. Delphes does write \texttt{Jet.Charge}; it was simply not carried into the
  ntuple schema. Now added, and it arrives with the next ntuplisation --- no
  re-simulation required.
\item[Decay modes and impact parameters.] Not representable: Delphes has no HPS decay
  modes and no track impact parameters. Both are acceptance-reducing on the CROWN side,
  so their absence also inflates our yield slightly.
\end{description}

\section{Two rows worth confirming}

Both are marked \qm\ above and both are one-line questions:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item Does CROWN apply a $\Delta R(\ell,\tauh)$ requirement at ntuple level? None was
  found in the configuration read; we apply $>0.5$, which would make us
  \emph{tighter} if CROWN does not.
\item What threshold does the extra-lepton veto use? CROWN's loose collections begin at
  $\pt>10$, whereas our veto only counts leptons above 20 --- so \textbf{we keep events
  CROWN vetoes}, making us looser on this row.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent Note that the two point in opposite directions, so they do not simply cancel:
one would tighten us and the other loosen us relative to CROWN.
